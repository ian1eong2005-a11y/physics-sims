5086 · 5a–5e · principle of moments

What to notice. Drag weights from the tray onto the beam. Each weight gives a moment = weight × distance from the pivot. The beam is balanced only when the total clockwise moment equals the total anticlockwise moment. Drag a weight off the beam to remove it.
