5086 · 10a–10d · order, uses and hazards

What to notice. Every region is the same kind of wave: transverse, and all travelling at the same speed in a vacuum (3 × 10⁸ m/s). Watch the race: the waves differ only in wavelength and frequency. Going from radio to gamma, the wavelength gets shorter and the frequency gets higher, and so does the energy carried, which is why the right-hand end is the dangerous end.
